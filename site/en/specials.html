FAQ

Questions?

Is the Special scary?

A little – with fog, pumpkins and skeletons. But there's nothing supernatural and no blood: every spook has an explanation, and the victim survives.

How is it different from the Solo cases?

It's bigger: four questions instead of three, a 50-minute countdown and new kinds of puzzles like the candy code. Otherwise it plays like Mordsteam Solo – on your own, without AI, on any device.

Can I play it after Halloween?

Yes. The code is valid for 12 months, and the case stays available after the season.

Can I pause?

No. The clock starts when you open the file and keeps running. Until then you can take as long as you like.

Can I give the case as a gift?

Yes. After buying you get a gift card to print or save as PDF. The name is only entered when playing.
