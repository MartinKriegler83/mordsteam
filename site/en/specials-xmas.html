Special · Christmas · Four Minutes Dark

“When the tree came on, the box was gone.”

Kerzenthal, on the Saturday before the third Sunday of Advent. At 18:00 the Christkind is due to switch on the big tree – and for that the whole Christmas market goes dark for four minutes. In the darkness the punch stall’s donation box disappears, and market manager Sepp Wimmer ends up in the butcher’s cold store. He is found much later, frozen to the bone but alive. The detectives are stuck in the snow on the mountain road. Until they arrive, you have visitors’ photos, the church clock, a group photo with punch mugs, a carton from the rubbish – and a keypad covered in icing sugar.

FAQ

Questions?

Is the case scary?

No, more cosy than creepy: a Christmas market, punch and snow – and a puzzle with a real bite. There’s no gore, and the victim survives.

How is it different from the Solo cases?

It’s bigger: four questions instead of three, a 50-minute countdown and new kinds of puzzle such as the template you slide across the market plan. Otherwise it plays just like Mordsteam Solo – on your own, without AI, on any device.

Can I play it after Christmas?

Yes. The code is valid for 12 months, and the case stays on sale after the season.

How do I give the case as a gift?

Tick “As a gift?” in the order form. After buying you get the gift card with your dedication to print or as a PDF – instantly, no shipping. The recipient enters their name only when they play.

Can I pause?

No. The clock starts as soon as you open the case file and keeps running. Until then, take as long as you like.

Can I play the case again?

Yes: up to three times within 30 days of your first playthrough. Culprit, time, hiding place and code are drawn anew each time, and the matching evidence changes too. Only your first playthrough counts for the comparison.

What is the €5 voucher?

After your first playthrough you get a voucher code worth €5 – whether you solved the case or not. Redeem it at checkout when ordering Mordsteam Friends or Teams. One voucher per order; it can’t be combined with Early Bird or other offers and can’t be exchanged for cash.
